## 2. What Was Completed in Session (v0.7.3)
1. **Root Cause Analysis & Fix for Price Discrepancy (105 vs 100 THB)**:
   - **Root Cause**:
     - In `OrderDetailPage.vue` line 156: calculated fixed fruit price as `(item.orderedKg || 1) * item.pricePerKg` (e.g. `3 * 35 = 105 บาท`), completely ignoring the bundle discount (`ชุด 3 กก. (100 บาท)`).
     - In `fruitStore.ts` (line 287) and `OrderDetailPage.vue` (line 560): when durian was weighed and saved, grand total recalculation iterated items using `(it.orderedKg || 1) * it.pricePerKg`, losing the bundle discount.
     - In `FruitSelector.vue`: when customer selected a bundle, `itemFinalPrice` was not set on the emitted `OrderItem`.
   - **Fix**:
     - Built centralized pricing engine `src/utils/pricing.ts` (`calculateItemSubtotal` and `calculateOrderFinalTotal`) that checks `item.itemFinalPrice`, parses `item.orderedBundle` (regex `/\d+\s*บาท/`), matches product catalog bundles, or falls back to `orderedKg * pricePerKg`.
     - In `FruitSelector.vue`, assigned `itemFinalPrice: matchingBundle ? matchingBundle.price : qty * prod.pricePerKg`.
     - In `fruitStore.ts` (`updateWeighedFruit`), `OrderDetailPage.vue` (`handleSaveWeight`, item display, `currentFinalPrice`), `AnalyticsPage.vue` (`fruitStats`, `orderAnalysisList`), and `IndexPage.vue` (`totalEstimatedPrice`), unified all subtotal calculations under `calculateItemSubtotal`.
2. **Durian Scale Combobox Selector with Strict Numeric Typing**:
   - Converted the inline weight input in `OrderDetailPage.vue` into an interactive combobox selector `<q-select>`:
     - `use-input`, `fill-input`, `hide-selected`, `behavior="menu"`, `options-dense`.
     - 30 predefined weight options (`1.5` - `4.0` in 0.1 kg increments + `4.2`, `4.5`, `5.0`).
     - Real-time search filter matching typed query against options.
     - Strict typing validation: `inputmode="decimal"`, `@keydown` blocking non-numeric keys, `@input-value` regex filtering (`[^0-9.]`) allowing only digits and at most one decimal point.
     - Enter key shortcut (`@keyup.enter="handleSaveWeight(idx)"`) and retained 6 quick preset chips (`2.0`, `2.4`, `2.6`, `2.8`, `3.0`, `3.2` kg).
